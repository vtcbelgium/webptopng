Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files or text uploaded to a remote server?

No. All computation occurs 100% client-side in your browser JavaScript thread. No payload data is ever transmitted across the network.

Does WebP & AVIF to PNG / JPG Converter use advertising or behavioral trackers?

Your input never leaves your browser. OTHRYS tools set no cookies and use no tracking pixels or third-party scripts; they only send an anonymous page-view count (no IP address or personal data stored).

Can I use this tool offline?

Yes! Once loaded, WebP & AVIF to PNG / JPG Converter executes without any network dependency. You can safely disconnect your internet connection and keep using it.
